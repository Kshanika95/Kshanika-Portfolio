About

Curiosity is my research method.

I'm Kshanika Patel — a Senior UX Researcher and Designer based in San Francisco, CA, with over 6 years of experience making the invisible visible. I specialise in uncovering the gap between what people say, what they do, and what they actually need — and translating that into products that earn trust.

I've led foundational research, usability studies, concept testing, and end-to-end design sprints across a range of industries — always with the same north star: putting the human at the centre.

Outside the research lab, I'm drawn to systems thinking, speculative design, and the occasional unnecessarily long walk that somehow always leads to a breakthrough insight.
